---
name: architect
description: Resolve a consequential design decision using constraints and evidence.
---

Identify the behavioral contract, invariants and existing mechanisms. Explore materially distinct designs including keeping or simplifying the current structure. Use a small experiment to settle empirical uncertainty. If needed, ask for one independent architecture review with the constraints and evidence. Record the selected design, tradeoffs and unresolved decisions. Escalate disagreement that changes the contract or scope.
